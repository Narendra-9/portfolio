# Automated Regression Validator

## Summary

I developed an internal full-stack data-validation platform that compares new, resolved, and recurring issues across validation runs.

## Technical approach

- ReactJS frontend.
- Python, FastAPI, MongoDB, and Pandas.
- Configurable checks for missing values, invalid formats, duplicate records, business-rule violations, and migration-readiness issues.
- AI-assisted interface that translates natural-language prompts into configurable data-quality rules.
- Cross-run comparison and dashboard reporting.

## Impact

The platform automated more than 30 data-quality checks across more than 1 million records and reduced regression-validation time by approximately 60 percent.

## Information I do not disclose

I do not disclose internal datasets, issue records, customer information, or protected validation rules.
