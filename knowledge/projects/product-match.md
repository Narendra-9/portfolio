# Product Match AI

## Summary

I independently built Product Match AI, a medical-device competitor-intelligence platform that helps analysts identify relevant competitor products and understand why they are relevant.

## My responsibility

I was the sole engineer for the full solution, including the React interface, FastAPI services, PostgreSQL and pgvector data layer, product-upload flow, search, match review, progress tracking, and structured exports.

## Technical approach

- ReactJS frontend.
- Python and FastAPI backend.
- PostgreSQL and pgvector.
- Multi-stage pipeline with metadata filtering, vector retrieval, cross-encoder reranking, LLM-assisted validation, and explainable result generation.

## Impact

The platform reduced analyst effort and time spent identifying relevant competitor products by approximately 50 percent.

## What I learned

I learned how to combine precision-oriented retrieval, validation, and explainability into a workflow that supports expert review rather than producing an opaque AI result.

## Information I do not disclose

I do not disclose the client's identity, product data, private evaluations, proprietary matching logic beyond this public description, or confidential business information.
