# Endava Solutions India

## Role and progression

I have worked at Endava Solutions India Private Limited since August 2024. I progressed from Associate Developer to Junior ML Engineer and then Machine Learning Engineer within two years.

My work combines AI engineering and hands-on full-stack delivery across healthcare, automation, enterprise intelligence, and employee-management products.

## Main responsibilities

- Translate business requirements into production-ready products.
- Build React interfaces and Python/FastAPI or Java/Spring services.
- Design retrieval, agentic workflows, APIs, data models, caching, and observability.
- Improve performance through query tuning, indexing, asynchronous processing, cache design, and connection management.
- Contribute throughout Agile delivery: requirements refinement, technical design, sprint planning, implementation, code review, debugging, integration testing, release coordination, and stakeholder collaboration.

## Selected work

### AI Companion

I owned the AI Companion as the sole engineer. I translated requirements into a React and FastAPI product with RAG, multi-agent orchestration, natural-language analytics, memory, tool integration, evaluation, and Redis caching. The AI services were deployed on AWS. Repeated analytical-request latency was reduced by approximately 80 percent.

### Product Match AI

I delivered Product Match AI independently as the sole engineer. It used React, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, and pgvector, with filtering, retrieval, reranking, LLM-assisted validation, and explainable results. The work reduced analyst research time spent identifying relevant competitor products by approximately 50 percent.

### Endava Talent Sphere

I built and internally delivered Endava Talent Sphere, a full-stack employee-management and workforce-insights platform using React, Spring WebFlux, PostgreSQL, and Caffeine caching. Query optimization, indexing, asynchronous processing, and connection management improved response latency from about 3 seconds to about 400 milliseconds.

### Automated Regression Validator

I developed the Automated Regression Validator using React, FastAPI, MongoDB, and Pandas. It compares new, resolved, and recurring issues across validation runs and provides configurable, AI-assisted data-quality rules. It supported more than 30 validation checks across more than 1 million records.

## What I learned

I learned to connect AI capability with end-to-end engineering: reliable data flows, measurable performance, clear product interfaces, and deliberate evaluation matter as much as the model itself.
