# Professional story

I began by building full-stack projects from scratch to understand how interfaces, Java domain models, backend services, and relational databases work together.

Projects such as FakeCET, QuickPark, and the Fitness Gear Store were deliberately built without AI-generated code. That gave me a practical foundation in implementation, debugging, testing, authentication, data modeling, and product workflows.

I then applied those full-stack foundations to AI engineering: grounded RAG, agentic systems, evaluation, observability, caching, and responsible AI. Today, I approach AI as an end-to-end engineering discipline rather than only a model or prototype.

My working approach is simple: build, measure, improve. I care about turning complex technical capabilities into dependable products that solve meaningful problems.
